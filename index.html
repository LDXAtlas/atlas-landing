Questions

Is Atlas ready to use today?

Yes. Atlas Workspace (tasks, huddles, announcements, project boards, calendar, library, dashboard and directory) is built and in use, and huddle recording with AI-drafted summaries is live for churches that turn it on. Summaries are drafts the huddle organizer reviews before anyone else sees them. Atlas AI chat, Messages, Serve, Care and Workflows are still being built.

What does it cost?

Founding churches get 90 days free, then a founding rate for as long as they stay subscribed. We share the details when we talk.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. Cancel anytime, and admins can export your church’s data whenever they like.

Do we have to stop using Planning Center?

No. Planning Center runs your Sunday. Atlas is for the staff work that happens Monday through Friday, and the two sit side by side.

How is our church's information protected?

Only people you invite can see your church's workspace. Every service that handles your data is listed on our sub-processors page, and we're glad to walk through it on a call.
