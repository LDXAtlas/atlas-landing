Data Processing Last Updated: October 15, 2026

List of Subprocessors

Last Updated: October 15, 2026

These are the third-party services that process personal data on behalf of Atlas Church Solutions. The AI providers only receive data from churches that have turned on Atlas AI. We give churches at least 30 days' notice before adding a new subprocessor, with the chance to object, as set out in our Data Processing Agreement.

Category Subprocessor Purpose & Details
Database, authentication and storage Supabase Database, user sign-in, file and huddle-recording storage. All Service data. United States (AWS us-west-2). Privacy policy
Hosting Vercel Hosts the Atlas app and this website. Request logs. United States. Privacy policy
Payments Stripe Subscriptions and card payments. Billing contact and payment details; Atlas never sees full card numbers. United States. Privacy policy
AI: transcription OpenAI Transcribes huddle audio, and provides backup text processing for some AI features. Only when a church has turned on Atlas AI. United States. Privacy policy
AI: summaries Anthropic Drafts huddle summaries, decisions, action items and follow-up flags from transcripts and notes. Only when a church has turned on Atlas AI. United States. Privacy policy
Email Resend Sends account, invitation and notification emails. Name, email address, message content. United States. Privacy policy
Website forms HubSpot Receives submissions from the forms on this website (not the app). Name, email, church, role, message. United States. Privacy policy

Database, authentication and storage

  • Supabase - Database, user sign-in, file and huddle-recording storage. All Service data. United States (AWS us-west-2).

Hosting

  • Vercel - Hosts the Atlas app and this website. Request logs. United States.

Payments

  • Stripe - Subscriptions and card payments. Billing contact and payment details; Atlas never sees full card numbers. United States.

AI: transcription

  • OpenAI - Transcribes huddle audio, and provides backup text processing for some AI features. Only when a church has turned on Atlas AI. United States.

AI: summaries

  • Anthropic - Drafts huddle summaries, decisions, action items and follow-up flags from transcripts and notes. Only when a church has turned on Atlas AI. United States.

Email

  • Resend - Sends account, invitation and notification emails. Name, email address, message content. United States.

Website forms

  • HubSpot - Receives submissions from the forms on this website (not the app). Name, email, church, role, message. United States.

Obligations

Note: All subprocessors are contractually obligated to protect personal data and comply with data protection laws including GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable regulations.

For questions about our subprocessors or to object to a new subprocessor, please contact privacy@atlaschurchsolutions.com.
